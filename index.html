Make it your game.

Names update immediately. The players swap roles every round: Player 1 describes Round 1, Player 2 describes Round 2, and so on. Each correct answer gives the current guesser 1 point. A wrong answer or timeout gives the current describer 1 point. Ten rounds; equal scores are a draw.

The describer keeps the phone for the entire round: reveal the picture, hide it, start the 1:30 answer timer, watch the clock, and tap Correct or False. The guesser answers aloud without taking the phone. Hand the phone to the other player only when the next round begins. Import a plain-text list with at least 10 image paths, one per line.
